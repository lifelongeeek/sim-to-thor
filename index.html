1Motivation

Too slow at the edge

GR00T N1.7 runs at about 8 Hz on Thor in BF16 (125 ms), while reactive manipulation needs 30 Hz or more.

Costly real data

Robot demonstrations are expensive, and plain simulation frames look visibly different from camera images.

Hard-to-trust evaluation

Real-robot results are slow to collect and hard to reproduce.

4.1 Data synthesis

  • Isaac Sim recreates LIBERO-style and SO-101 move-the-cube scenes with randomized objects, lighting and layout.
  • Cosmos 3 turns sim frames into camera-like images to shrink the visual sim-to-real gap.
  • Evaluation scenes are held out from all training scenes.

4.2 Policy compression

Step distillation (Drift)

A flow-matching action head integrates the velocity field over several denoising steps. We distill it into a single step with a drift loss: each generated action is pulled toward the demonstration while sibling samples of the same input push each other apart, which keeps the action distribution diverse without a teacher at inference time.

  • GR00T N1.7: the DiT action head runs once instead of 4 times.
  • Cosmos3-Edge: the 4-step sampler with classifier-free guidance (8 tower forwards) becomes 1 forward at σ = 1 with guidance off.

Vision token pruning / video token drop

Most of the sequence entering the backbone is visual. We remove the tokens the action does not need.

  • GR00T N1.7 · Grid-Sampler: a small head predicts K continuous coordinates per camera and bilinearly samples K tokens from the vision-encoder patch grid, so only K image tokens reach the LLM.
  • Cosmos3-Edge · video token drop: the world-action model also generates future video (8 latent frames × 340 tokens). We keep the conditioning frame and the first K generated frames and drop the rest; dropping 75% keeps 2 of 8 frames, dropping 100% keeps actions only (FastWAM).

NVFP4 quantization

Mixed NVFP4/FP8 TensorRT engines, calibrated per component: vision encoder in FP8, LLM in NVFP4 with o_proj/down_proj in FP8, DiT action head in NVFP4.

4.3 Real-time control on Thor

  • Per-component TensorRT engines captured in CUDA Graphs remove launch overhead.
  • Async execution: the robot runs chunk k while Thor computes chunk k+1; RTC/LIPO keeps transitions smooth.

7Limitations & next steps

  • Real-robot results cover one task (move-the-cube) on SO-101.
  • One-step distillation still loses success on complex RoboLab tasks; combining it with video token drop needs further training.
  • Next: Cosmos 3 planning on B300 with the Thor policy for long-horizon tasks.
